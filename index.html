What Helix brings to a pitcher

A measurement and training stack for Jaden, who would be a private pro client rather than a public pitching offer. Helix measures and hosts. The Rockies' pitching, strength and medical staff can provide direction and receive feedback, at Jaden's discretion.

Axioforce pitching mound

A custom force-plate mound: how hard the drive leg pushes, how firmly the lead leg blocks, and the timing between them, on every throw off the mound. A weak or late lead-leg block pushes load up the chain to the arm, so it ties straight into both his shoulder health and his command.

Qualisys Miqus motion capture, 9 cameras

Full 3D delivery capture tied to TrackMan: sequencing, trunk rotation timing, arm path, and how the shoulder is loaded, lined up pitch by pitch with what the ball did.

Theia markerless capture

No markers, so it runs on every throwing session at Helix. It catches mechanics drifting as he tires, which is the likely link between his shoulder and his walks.

Axioforce readiness tests

Jump, isometric and landing tests every two weeks. They show readiness, left/right asymmetry and lower-body power as strength work builds.

TrackMan ball tracking

Every bullpen: velocity, spin, movement, extension and release-point consistency, the best measurable proxy for command. It also shows whether the sinker can be separated from the four-seam, and whether the slider's shape can gain sweep and be located better to lefties.

Live hitters in the building

Helix's hitting clients give him live at-bats in late January, and our hitters get reps against big-league stuff. One TrackMan session covers both.

iPads + lab computing

Session video and a short weekly report to Jaden, shared with the Rockies' staff if he chooses.

A pitching coach and a physical therapist of Jaden's choosing work alongside Helix. Location: Helix is about 30 minutes from his off-season home, more with traffic.
